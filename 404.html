THE STORY BEHIND ENVELOPE

The problem I experienced

Our budget lived in
WhatsApp messages.

My partner and I already used the cash-envelope method, but the digital part was completely scattered. A deposit was a message. A bill payment was a screenshot. The balance was mental math.

Scattered conversations

Every expense was buried between regular WhatsApp messages, photos, and screenshots. Finding an older payment meant scrolling through weeks of chat.

+ −No running total

We knew money had moved, but neither of us could instantly answer how much was still available in the Amex, Food, or House envelope.

Two different pictures

One person might pay a bill and forget to message the other. Our decisions were shared, but our records were not.

Goals felt invisible

We were contributing to savings, but without visible progress the goal never felt close—or rewarding—to either of us.

02

How the idea started

What if the physical envelopes we trusted had a shared digital home?

The idea did not start with another budgeting dashboard. It started with the method that was already working for us: giving every dollar a purpose before spending it.

I wanted to preserve that simple mental model, then remove the friction around it. Each category would still feel like its own envelope—but both partners could add money, record payments, and see the balance change immediately.

Questions, answered

Before you open
your first envelope.

Is this connected to our bank?

No. You manually add deposits and payments, just like using physical cash envelopes—but faster and with a current balance.

Can both partners use it?

Yes. Every update is attributed to Maya or Arjun, making it easy to understand who added or spent money.

Can we have personal envelopes too?

Yes. Shared goals can sit beside personal envelopes such as Maya’s Spa Fund or Arjun’s Cricket Bat Fund.

Can we keep a monthly record?

Yes. Export creates an Excel workbook with a summary, every update, and a separate sheet for each envelope.
